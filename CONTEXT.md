# Miau

Miau turns a vocal performance into cat meowing that follows the singer's musical expression.

## Language

**Vocal performance**:
The singer's input, whose pitch, timing, and loudness guide the cat performance. Its words are not preserved in the cat performance.

**Cat performance**:
A sequence of real recorded meows following the vocal performance's pitch, timing, and loudness.
_Avoid_: Cartoon voice, pitch-shifted voice

**Meow sample**:
A recording of a real cat's meow used as the sound source for a cat performance.

**Vocal clip**:
A recorded vocal performance supplied by a visitor to make a cat performance.

**Cat audio**:
The downloadable recording of a cat performance generated from a vocal clip.
