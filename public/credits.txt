audio/meow-pleading-1.wav through audio/meow-pleading-5.wav
Five separate meow utterances from "Meow of a pleading cat" by Heismark,
8 October 2010. Released into the public domain by its author (PD-self).
Source and license declaration:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Meow_of_a_pleading_cat.oga
Original audio:
https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/6/6b/Meow_of_a_pleading_cat.oga
Edits: decoded to mono 24 kHz PCM WAV, isolated the following source intervals,
and added brief edge fades to avoid clicks. These are different recorded calls,
not pitch-shifted duplicates of the original Siamese sample.
1: 0.24–1.64 seconds (10 ms edge fades)
2: 3.50–4.97 seconds (10 ms edge fades)
3: 6.63–7.79 seconds (10 ms edge fades)
4: 9.30–10.17 seconds (20 ms edge fades)
5: 10.17–11.48 seconds (20 ms edge fades)
