# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated by the user's instruction to build quickly without more questions. Use plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with Vite for local development and static builds; a worker handles audio analysis and rendering.

## Users

People receiving a link from a friend who want to hear their vocal recording performed by a cat.

## Product Purpose

An amusing, shareable upload-to-download vocal-to-cat demo. Success means a visitor can quickly obtain recognizable cat meows following their recording.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Upload a short vocal recording, generate a sample-based cat performance, preview it, and download WAV audio. Preserve approximate musical expression, not words. Process on the visitor's device. No live input or plugin is needed for the first version.

## Product Principles

- Make the sound recognizable and entertaining before pursuing transcription accuracy.
- Keep the first interaction obvious and short.
- Use real recorded meows.
- Prioritize a quick useful version over production-music features.

## Open Decisions and Assumptions

Miau is the working name inferred from the project folder. A 60-second clip limit and single-voice quality target were included in the approved MVP. Visual direction and hosting provider are delegated implementation choices, not user-pinned brand commitments.
